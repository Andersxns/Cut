Cut Browser 1.1 adds **Tor windows**, like Brave's private windows with Tor. It's built on Firefox <!-- firefox --> and Tor from Tor Browser <!-- tor -->.

## Which file do I need?

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Cut-Browser-1.1.0-Setup-x64.exe` |
| Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint, Pop!_OS | the `amd64.deb`: `sudo apt install ./cut-browser_*.deb` |
| Any other Linux (64-bit) | the `.tar.xz`: unpack it and run `./install.sh` |

Installing over 1.0 keeps your bookmarks, history and settings. <!-- signing --> `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of every file.

## What's new

- **Tor windows** (menu › **New Tor window**, or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>): everything in a Tor window goes through the Tor network. Sites can't see your IP address, your internet provider can't see which sites you visit, and each site gets a Tor circuit of its own. Tor windows are always private, share nothing with your other windows, and search with Cut Search through Tor.
- **.onion sites** open in a Tor window from anywhere. Addresses you type in a normal window go straight there; when a page links to one, a bar offers to open it (so no page can send your Tor window somewhere by itself), and **Open Link in New Tor Window** is on every link's menu.
- **.onion in the address bar** when a site has an .onion address of its own (Onion-Location), as in Tor Browser and Brave. In Tor windows, Cut Browser can switch to it by itself (Settings › Cut Browser).
- **New identity** and **New Tor circuit for this site**, from the Tor button beside the address bar.
- **Bridges**, where Tor is blocked: the built-in obfs4, Snowflake and meek bridges, or bridges of your own (Settings › Cut Browser).
- Tor and its bridge client come with Cut Browser, so nothing is downloaded the first time you open a Tor window.

Tor windows hide where you are, but they aren't Tor Browser, which also makes everyone look the same to the sites they visit. When your safety depends on it, use [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/download/).

Also in this release:

- **Firefox's new look.** Firefox redesigned its interface in version 157. Cut Browser's interface has been updated for it: the address bar floats in the middle of the window again, and Firefox's new purple tints and gradient give way to Cut's own colors, in the browser and in Settings.
- **Works on more sites.** Cut Browser now introduces itself to sites as Firefox, so sites that only let in browsers they recognize, such as YouTube Music, work. (Before, it called itself "Cut", which some sites turned away.)
- **More accurate searches.** In Cut Search, results that mention more of your search rank higher, and an engine that answers for only one word of it is left out: "overtime in rocket league" finds Rocket League's overtime, not overtime pay. Wikipedia suggests only articles about your search's words.
- uBlock Origin also runs in private windows (and Tor windows).

## Updates

Cut Browser doesn't update itself from Mozilla (that would turn it back into plain Firefox). New versions are published here when Firefox or Tor releases security fixes; install a new version over the old one and your bookmarks, history and settings stay.
