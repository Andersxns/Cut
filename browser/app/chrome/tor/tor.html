Starting Tor…

  • Your IP address stays hidden Sites see a Tor address instead of yours. Your internet provider can see that you use Tor, but not where you go.
  • .onion sites work And each site gets a Tor circuit of its own, so no single relay sees all the sites you visit.
  • Nothing is kept History, cookies and site data are deleted when you close the last Tor window.

Tor windows hide where you are, but they aren't Tor Browser, which also makes everyone look the same to the sites they visit. When your safety depends on it, use Tor Browser.
